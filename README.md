## About

I'm **Suraj Pandey**, a software engineer and founder building products at the intersection of software, privacy, and intelligent systems.

My work spans:

- 🔐 End-to-end encryption & privacy-preserving systems
- 🧠 AI/ML integration and on-device intelligence
- ⚡ Real-time communication & distributed systems
- 📱 iOS, Android, React Native & web applications
- 🔎 Search and information discovery
- 🦀 Rust systems & cryptographic primitives
- ☁️ Cloud infrastructure and backend architecture
- 🎨 Product engineering from 0 → production

I enjoy working on problems where **the engineering underneath the product is just as interesting as the product itself.**

## 🚀 Products & Projects

### 🌐 Vync

**A community-driven social platform built around privacy, identity choice and real-time interaction.**

Vync combines social communities with **Public and Anonymous modes**, live rooms, audio clubs, livestreaming, messaging, creator rewards and real-time experiences.

**Highlights**

- Public + Anonymous social modes
- Privacy-focused identity architecture
- Communities & social feeds
- Audio clubs & live rooms
- Livestreaming with multi-host support
- Real-time reactions
- Creator gifting & rewards
- AI-powered content moderation
- Recommendation systems

`Swift` `Kotlin` `SwiftUI` `Jetpack Compose` `Go` `Django` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `OpenSearch` `WebRTC` `LiveKit` `Docker` `Kubernetes` `AWS`

---

### 🔐 Sanchr

**Privacy-first communication and secure data infrastructure.**

Sanchr is focused on building a deeper privacy architecture around encrypted communication, secure media, contact protection and local vaults.

**Highlights**

- End-to-end encrypted communication
- Encrypted media & contacts
- Secure local vault
- Sanchr Mode
- Cryptographic identity
- Privacy-preserving primitives
- Rust-based security components

`Rust` `Ed25519` `Curve25519` `Argon2` `PSI` `OPRF` `gRPC`

---

### 🗣️ Tarka

**A privacy-first browser experience for real-time captions and translation.**

Tarka brings native speech understanding directly into the browsing experience, including captions and real-time translation for video, audio and calls.

The goal is simple:

> **Understand what you're watching or hearing without having to give away your voice data.**

**Highlights**

- Real-time captions
- Real-time translation
- Works with video, audio and calls
- Local/on-device speech processing
- Privacy-focused architecture
- Designed for platforms such as YouTube, Zoom, Teams and more

`Rust` `C/C++` `Speech AI` `On-device ML` `macOS` `Browser Technologies`

---

### 🔎 Shodhya

**A privacy-first search engine built around useful discovery without tracking.**

Shodhya explores a different approach to search — focused on finding information while keeping the experience clean and privacy-conscious.

**Principles**

- No tracking
- No ads
- Privacy-first search
- Fast information discovery
- Designed for the modern web

`Search` `AI` `Information Retrieval` `Web Infrastructure`

---

### 📱 LunaBloom

**A privacy-first personal tracking application designed to keep sensitive data local.**

LunaBloom is built around the idea that highly personal information should not need to leave the device.

- Offline-first architecture
- No unnecessary network communication
- Local data storage
- Privacy-focused design

`Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `Room` `Android`

---

### 🏋️ Vitara

**An AI-powered fitness companion.**

Vitara explores computer vision and on-device intelligence to provide movement analysis and adaptive fitness experiences.

- Pose detection
- Exercise recognition
- Adaptive coaching
- On-device processing

`SwiftUI` `HealthKit` `MediaPipe` `Computer Vision`

---

### 🔗 QRBOB

**A modern QR generation and sharing platform.**

QRBOB focuses on making QR creation simple while providing useful tools around links, sharing and analytics.

`Next.js` `React` `QR` `Analytics`

---

### 🎓 Homeworkly

**A student-focused productivity and learning application.**

Built to simplify everyday academic workflows and provide students with a lightweight digital workspace.

`React Native` `Expo` `Supabase`
